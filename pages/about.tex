\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this document}

This is a condensed version of the thesis, prepared for discussion with
the supervisor and the advisor. It follows the structure the thesis will
have and contains its actual content, reduced to the essentials: each
section states what it establishes, in a few paragraphs instead of the
full text.

The research questions, the formal problem definition, the contributions
and all reported results are final. The numbers come from four parameter
sweeps, 348 simulation runs, and every table names the files it was
generated from.

Two things are still outstanding. Figures are shown as framed
placeholders describing what each one will contain, and the front matter,
the appendix material and the AI usage disclosure are listed but not
written. The bibliography is in place; the entries marked in
\texttt{bibliography.bib} as unverified still need a DBLP check.
