\chapter{Additional material}

\section{Full parameter table}

The complete configuration of the simulator with its baseline value for
every parameter, placed next to the parameter table of the reference paper
so that the two can be compared line by line.

\section{Per-seed result tables}

Chapter~\ref{chapter:experiments} reports means over five seeds. This
section gives the per-seed rows for all five sweeps, from which the
seed-to-seed variation behind those means can be checked.

\section{Full grids}

The complete blocking-counter grid behind Table~\ref{tab:blocking}, the
complete smoothing-factor grid behind Table~\ref{tab:alpha-grid}, and the
full adaptive-versus-random cell grid behind Table~\ref{tab:x3-matched},
including the twelve engaged cells that no random cell matches to within
the tolerance.

\section{Implementation defects found and fixed}

The defects surfaced while hardening the received code, each with its
symptom, its fix, and the metric it would otherwise have distorted.

\section{Supplemental material and AI usage disclosure}

The repository containing the simulator, the experiment runner scripts and the
exported result files, followed by a disclosure of which AI tools were
used and how, separated into conceptualising, developing code, writing and
proofreading.
